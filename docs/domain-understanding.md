# Domain Understanding
---

## Core Concepts

### Policy
Defines what services are covered and under what conditions.

May contain:
- coverage percentages
- annual limits
- deductibles
- exclusions

### Claim
A reimbursement request submitted by a member.

### Claim Line Item
Individual service entries within a claim.

Each line item may be adjudicated separately.

### Adjudication
The process of determining:
- whether a claim is covered
- payable amount
- denial reasons
- whether manual review is needed

---

## Important Behaviors

### Partial Approvals
Claims may contain mixed outcomes:
- approved items
- denied items
- manual review items

### Annual Limits
Policies may restrict total yearly coverage amounts.

### Manual Review
High-cost or unusual claims may require human intervention.

### Explanations
System should explain approval/denial decisions clearly.
